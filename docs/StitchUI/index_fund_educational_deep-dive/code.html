Understanding Index Funds

Why buying the whole basket is often better than trying to pick the perfect apple.

How it Works

Instead of buying stock in one company (like Apple or Tesla), you buy a tiny slice of hundreds of companies at once. This strategy minimizes risk through diversification.

The Upside

The Downside

  • cancel
    Average Returns You won't "beat the market" because you own the market.
  • cancel
    Lack of Control You can't exclude specific companies from the index.
